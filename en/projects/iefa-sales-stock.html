Sales and stock system for a charity shop

A point-of-sale and stock system that the volunteers of IEFA's shop use on every working day, built by a team of eight UNIVESP students.

My part: I worked on every stage with the team: the stock count and product registration, the Django models and MySQL database, the point of sale, the reports and labels, the installation on the shop's computer and the follow-up with the volunteers, including the stock-exit fix.

Team code, not public

100%of the planned features delivered; in use on every working day of the shop since about July 2026

Decision it supports
How much was sold, by which payment method, and what is in stock.
Who uses it
Volunteers and coordinators of a charity shop.
What an error costs
Wrong prices at the counter and untracked stock become lost money and hard-to-close accounts.
How it creates value
A barcode sale updates stock by itself and the report by payment method closes the day; reports of wrong prices stopped.
Risks and limits
A shared login and manual tests; there is no cash-register opening and closing.

Problem

The shop of the Instituto Educacional Francisco de Assis, in São José do Rio Preto, recorded sales on paper and in notebooks and had no structured stock control. Not every item carried its price, which led to wrong prices at the counter.

Solution

A Django and MySQL system with a barcode point of sale, products, stock movements, payment methods, users and sales reports, installed on the shop's Windows computer as a service. Every sale writes its stock exit automatically, and new stock prints its barcode labels.

How it works

  • The team counted the whole stock, registered every product and printed the labels before go-live.
  • Watching the volunteers use it added features nobody had asked for: remove an item, cancel a sale, initial stock in the product form and search everywhere.
  • A defect in the stock exits of sales was fixed in the code and the missing movements were repaired with a SQL script.

Results

Results from the final report delivered to UNIVESP
0reports of wrong or missing prices since the barcode labels were adopted
9modules in production: sales, point of sale, stock, products, categories, payments, users, reports, labels
2reports the volunteers use in their routine: sales by payment method and label printing

Limits

Results are observed, not measured: there is no before-and-after count of errors. Tests are manual, and the volunteers share one login, so actions are not traceable to a person. The code belongs to the team and is not public.
