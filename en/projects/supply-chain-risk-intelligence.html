Problem

“We are often late” is not something operations can act on. They need the cause, the timing, the volume coming, the orders at risk and what to change.

Solution

Five connected analyses of 180,519 real orders: propensity score matching, survival models, demand forecasting, a calibrated risk model explained with SHAP, and a linear program that turns the risk scores into a cheaper shipping allocation.

How it works

  • CI downloads the real dataset (checksum-pinned), validates it against a data contract, re-runs the notebooks and fails if any published number moves.
  • Causal effect estimated on 35,216 matched pairs, all 78 covariate levels balanced.
  • The optimization uses out-of-sample risk scores and is stress-tested with 10,000 Monte Carlo runs.

Results

Measured results, from the repository
1.99 dayslater delivery for Second Class than Standard Class, against the promise
33%lower forecast error than the naive baseline (MAPE 5.9% vs 8.9%)
94.4%of the top 10% riskiest orders are late (AUC 0.73)

Limits

The optimization saves 0.85% of expected cost on orders the model never saw: real, but small, and reported as such.
