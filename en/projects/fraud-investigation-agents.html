Fraud investigation agents

A team of agents that investigates card-fraud alerts and has its reports checked against the evidence.

Code

83%fraud patterns named correctly, against 40% for a single model call

Decision it supports
For each fraud alert: block the card or clear the purchase, and why.
Who uses it
Fraud prevention analysts, who today investigate each alert by hand.
What an error costs
Clearing a fraud costs the purchase amount; blocking a legitimate customer costs friction and sometimes the customer.
How it creates value
The agents do the repetitive investigation and hand over a report with the evidence. The analyst reviews instead of starting from scratch, and the critic stops an unsupported report from clearing a fraud.
Risks and limits
Measured on synthetic data; the critic does not check the free-text summary, so the final decision stays with the analyst.

Problem

Agents that investigate fraud alerts are easy to demo and hard to trust: they can name a pattern the data does not show, skip a check, or clear a fraudulent purchase in confident prose.

Solution

A LangGraph team. A planner chooses which checks to run, a researcher runs them through two MCP servers (card activity and the fraud model), a reporter writes a verdict that cites the evidence, and a rule-based critic rejects reports the evidence does not support and sends the work back. It runs on a local 3B model on a free CPU runner.

How it works

  • Code fetches every number; the language models only decide what to check and what it means.
  • The critic is rules over the evidence, so its decisions are reproducible and explainable.
  • Measured on 30 alerts with known answers against the fraud model alone, plain rules and a single LLM call.

Results

Measured results, from the repository
83%fraud pattern right, against 40% for a single LLM call
27 of 30final summaries that state only what the evidence shows, against 50% for a single call
7 of 7legitimate purchases cleared, against 2 of 7 in the first evaluation run

Limits

The data is synthetic. The critic's checks on the summary are lexical, and three reports stayed rejected because the 3B reporter could not write a summary it accepts.

Stack

LangGraph, MCP, Ollama, XGBoost, pytest
