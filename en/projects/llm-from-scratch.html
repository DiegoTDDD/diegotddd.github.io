LLM from scratch

A GPT-style model written from scratch and trained on 10-K filings on a free CPU runner.

Problem

Most LLM projects call an API or fine-tune someone else's model. When something breaks, such as a tokenizer mismatch, a cache bug or a sampling setting, you need to understand the parts.

Solution

Every part of a decoder-only transformer built and tested on its own: byte-level BPE, causal self-attention, the training loop, sampling and KV-cache decoding, trained on Business, Risk Factors and MD&A sections of 10-K filings.

How it works

  • Each component has its own tests, including a naive BPE as a reference for the fast one.
  • Training runs on the free GitHub Actions runner, so anyone can reproduce it.
  • KV-cache decoding is checked to produce the same tokens as full recomputation, also past the context with a sliding window; fused attention is measured against the written-out one.
