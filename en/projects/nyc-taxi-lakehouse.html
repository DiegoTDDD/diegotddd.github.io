NYC taxi lakehouse

A Delta Lake lakehouse over 42 million trips, processed month by month, that separates the congestion fee's effect from winter.

CodeLive demo

−0.4 ptschange in Manhattan trips per day due to the fee, once winter is taken out; the raw drop was 10.7%

Decision it supports
Assessing whether a public policy (the congestion fee) changed behaviour.
Who uses it
Policy, mobility and urban planning analysts.
What an error costs
Concluding from dirty or incomplete data leads to a wrong policy assessment.
How it creates value
With the same months a year earlier as the control, the 10.7% drop in Manhattan trips turns out to be almost all winter (−0.4 points due to the fee); the fee's effect is in trips that got shorter in time (−3.6 points) over the same distance.
Risks and limits
Assumes winter changes taxi use the same way every year; other changes between 2024 and 2025 are not separated.

Problem

Whether New York's 2025 congestion fee changed taxi use: the fee started in January, so a simple before/after mixes it with winter, and the trip files change schema mid-way and hold invalid records.

Solution

Bronze, Silver and Gold layers in Delta Lake over two Oct-Mar seasons, transformed with DuckDB and orchestrated by Dagster as monthly partitions. The estimate subtracts what winter did in the season before the fee, and eleven data-quality checks stop bad data before it reaches the numbers.

How it works

  • Each month is a Dagster partition written with a predicate overwrite: added or redone alone, and redoing it changes nothing.
  • Tests prove month-by-month and all-at-once builds give identical Gold tables.
  • The same months a year earlier, without the fee, are the control for the season.
  • Blocking quality checks stop the run before bad rows reach Gold.

Results

Measured results, from the repository
−0.4 ptstrips per day: the 10.7% raw drop is almost all winter
−3.6 ptstrip duration attributable to the fee, with distance unchanged
42.1Mtrips in twelve monthly partitions

Limits

Assumes winter changes taxi use the same way every year; yellow taxis only.

Stack

Delta Lake, DuckDB, Dagster, Streamlit
